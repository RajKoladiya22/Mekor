PP-R Piping System

A lightweight piping system made of PP-R copolymer material, with high mechanical strength and resistance to corrosion. It is made from the latest scientific formula with high-quality polypropylene random copolymer, it's non-toxic, ensuring safe drinking water for both residential and commercial buildings.

  • Light in weight
  • Hygienic and non-toxic
  • Outstanding heat insulation
  • Smooth inner wall
  • Safe homogeneous connections

About Plastics

Plastics are polymers created by the chemical conversion of natural products or synthesized from organic materials. The primary components are long chains of carbon (C) and hydrogen (H) known as monomers. The raw materials used are natural compounds such as cellulose, coal, oil and natural gas.

Metal

  • High density: Crane is needed for transport. Requires wide spacing for fixings.
  • Thermal conductivity: Insulation is needed to limit heat loss.
  • Corrosion Behaviors: Galvanic corrosion can occur, reducing internal diameter and causing pressure losses.
  • Chemical resistance: Low Resistance to Acids and damage from Incrustation.

Plastic

  • Low density: Can be carried by hand up to d110. Requires minimal spacing for fixings. Simple and economical.
  • Low thermal conductivity: Limited heat loss. Low levels of condensation and resistance to corrosion.
  • High Corrosion Resistance: Galvanic Corrosion Free. Prevents diameter reduction. No pressure losses.
  • High chemical resistance: A minimum of 25-years of life with correct jointing methods. Incrustation free.

Carbon Footprint of Plastics Vs Metal
