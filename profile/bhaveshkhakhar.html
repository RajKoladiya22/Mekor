About Us

Engineered Piping Solutions
for Industrial & Infrastructure Projects

Mekor Pipes designs and manufactures high-performance pipes and fittings for water, oil & gas, chemical processing and infrastructure. We combine materials engineering, process control and precision fabrication to deliver durable, code-compliant piping systems optimized for your application.

What We Do

Industrial Pipe Manufacturing

High-strength pipes manufactured to ASTM, EN and custom specifications for water, wastewater, oil, gas and chemical transport.

Custom Fittings & Fabrication

Tailored fittings, flanges and fabricated assemblies. Small runs and large projects handled with CNC and skilled welding.

Quality Assurance & Testing

Material certification, hydrostatic testing, NDT and dimensional inspection to ensure traceable quality and regulatory compliance.

Engineering & Project Support

Technical drawings, material selection, on-site assistance and lifecycle guidance to reduce installation risk and long-term maintenance costs.
